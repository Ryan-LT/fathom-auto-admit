# Privacy Policy: Fathom Auto-Admit for Google Meet

This extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- It runs only on `https://meet.google.com/*`, where it reads the waiting list to find the bot names you configured and clicks Admit for them.
- Your settings (on/off, bot names, admitted count) are stored with `chrome.storage.sync` in your own browser profile.
- It makes no network requests and uses no analytics or remote code.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
